J Harmony membership

J Harmony is a proactive medical care club based in Buena Park, California, with treatment locations in Beverly Hills and Los Angeles. One annual membership combines concierge medical access, hormone optimization, medical aesthetics, and a private member portal that charts labs, visits, and wearable trends. Inquire at (714) 983-4298 or info@j-harmony.com.

What the membership includes

  • Personal access to the J Harmony care team, without a traditional waiting-room model.
  • Hormone optimization that is personalized and followed over time.
  • Medical aesthetics, including Alma Hybrid skin resurfacing.
  • A private portal that brings together blood and diagnostic results, in-person visit findings, and wearable readings. Screens on the site show sample data. AI-generated insights are reviewed by J Harmony medical staff before they reach the member.

Who it is for

  • Adults who want concierge-style medical access, hormone care, and aesthetics coordinated in one membership rather than booked as separate visits across clinics.
  • Membership is by inquiry. J Harmony does not publish a public price on j-harmony.com.

Questions

Is J Harmony a med spa or a medical clinic?

J Harmony describes itself as a proactive medical care club. The membership covers concierge medical care, hormone optimization, longevity monitoring, and medical aesthetics. Care is delivered by board-certified family nurse practitioners Regine Lapitan, FNP-C and Kacy Voltmer, FNP-C.

Headquarters is 6061 Dale St, Ste C, Buena Park, CA 90621. Treatment locations are 433 N Camden Dr, Ste 610, Beverly Hills, CA 90210, and 11620 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90025.
