Concierge medicine at J Harmony

J Harmony provides concierge medical access as part of an annual membership, with locations in Buena Park, Beverly Hills, and Los Angeles. Members get direct access to the care team, visits, lab review, and a portal that tracks bloodwork, in-person findings, and wearable data. Call (714) 983-4298 to inquire.

How concierge access works here

  • The membership is built around personal access rather than a walk-in med-spa schedule. The site describes no waiting rooms and care that is planned and followed by the member's team.
  • Kacy Voltmer, FNP-C sees members for proactive care and reviews labs, visits, and trends. Regine Lapitan, FNP-C cares for members across wellness and aesthetics.
  • Three data sources feed the member portal: lab panels charted against the lab's own ranges, vitals and findings from visits, and day-to-day readings from devices the member already wears.

What it is not

  • J Harmony is a membership club, not an emergency department or a hospital. It does not publish insurance billing details on its website. Ask during the membership inquiry what is included.

Questions

Does J Harmony take walk-in aesthetics appointments?

The public site presents care as an annual membership with inquiry, not as a published walk-in menu. Start with the membership conversation at (714) 983-4298.

Is there a patient portal?

Yes. Members sign in at app.j-harmony.com. The site says the portal is powered by HIPAA-compliant AI and that insights are reviewed by medical staff.
