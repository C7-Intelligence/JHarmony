Hormone replacement therapy

J Harmony includes hormone optimization in its annual medical membership. Plans are personalized and continuously monitored, with lab trends such as total testosterone charted in the member portal and reviewed by the care team. Clinics are in Buena Park, Beverly Hills, and Los Angeles. Call (714) 983-4298.

What the site says about hormone care

  • Hormone replacement therapy is listed as a member service: optimization that is personalized and continuously monitored.
  • The member portal shows hormone trends. A published sample view charts total testosterone. Sample screens are labeled as sample data.
  • A published member case pairs physician-guided GLP-1 therapy with hormone replacement. That member was down 35 pounds at 90 days, with progress continuing through five months. Individual results vary.

Who reviews the plan

  • Regine Lapitan, FNP-C and Kacy Voltmer, FNP-C are the named clinicians. Both are board-certified family nurse practitioners. Kacy reviews labs, visits, and trends to keep each plan on track.

Questions

Do I need labs before hormone therapy?

The membership includes lab panels charted against the lab's own ranges, and the care team reviews those results. A specific panel is set during the membership assessment, not on the public site.

Is hormone therapy only for men?

The public site does not limit hormone optimization to one sex. Eligibility is determined in the membership assessment.
