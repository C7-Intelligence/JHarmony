Aesthetic technology

Alma Hybrid laser resurfacing

J Harmony treats with the Alma Hybrid, a skin resurfacing platform that pairs an ablative CO2 laser with a non-ablative 1570 nm laser. The provider sets the blend for the member's skin, from a light refresh to deeper resurfacing. Treatment locations are in Beverly Hills, Buena Park, and Los Angeles. Call (714) 983-4298.

What it is used for

  • Resurfacing for texture, tone, fine lines, and sun damage.
  • Repair of scars, including acne scarring.
  • Treatments are scheduled and followed by the care team. The ProScan handpiece places each laser pulse in a precise, repeatable pattern.

A published result

  • The site shows a member 60 days after two Alma Hybrid fractional CO2 treatments. Final results typically continue developing over 3 to 6 months. Another member reported skin repair underway after 3 treatments in Beverly Hills. Individual results vary.

Questions

Is Alma Hybrid the same as a traditional CO2 laser?

The Alma Hybrid pairs an ablative CO2 laser with a non-ablative 1570 nm laser. The blend is set per treatment rather than using CO2 alone.
